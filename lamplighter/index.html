What goes in

Public data, every skater

MoneyPuck game logs and shot data: ice time, power-play time, expected goals per 60, shots and finishing, plus the opponent's defense, the starting goalie, home ice and back-to-backs.

The math

A chance, not a hunch

Expected goals feed a Poisson model, so the chance of at least one goal is 1 − e−λ. Points work the same way. Shots use a negative binomial, which allows for streaky nights.

Where it's honest

Tested, then graded

Every morning new ideas are tested on games the model never saw, and one is adopted only if it wins on two separate test windows. Every logged pick is graded from box scores and stays on the record.

Questions

Is this betting advice?

No. These are model estimates for research and entertainment. They'll be wrong plenty of nights, and past results don't predict future ones. Only bet where it's legal, only if you're 21 or older, and only what you can afford to lose.

What does the percentage mean?

It's the model's chance that the player does it at least once tonight: scores a goal, records a point, or reaches the shot line. So far those percentages have matched what actually happened within a couple of points.

When are picks posted?

Before the first puck drop of each slate, after starting goalies are checked. If a goalie changes late, the board can shift, so check it again near game time.

How do I get access after paying?

Click Sign in, approve Lamplighter on Whop with the same account you paid with, and the full board unlocks on this site. Access is checked with Whop every few minutes.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans are managed in your Whop account, and you keep access through the end of the period you paid for. Day passes don't renew.

Why do you show the misses?

Because a record that only shows wins isn't a record. Every logged pick stays on this page, hit or miss.
