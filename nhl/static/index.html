What the number means

Goal chance is the probability a skater scores at least one goal tonight, including overtime. Fair odds convert that probability into American odds with no bookmaker margin, so a sportsbook price longer than the fair price is the one worth a look.

What goes into it

Each skater's shot rate and shooting percentage, pulled toward the league average for their position so small samples don't produce wild numbers. That rate is applied to tonight's projected ice time, with power-play minutes weighted heavily, then adjusted for the confirmed opposing goalie, how many shots the opponent allows, and home ice.

Why you can trust the percentages

Tested on 10,115 player-games from the 2025-26 season using only data available before each game. When the model said 10–13%, players scored about 10% of the time; in its top tier it predicted 31.5% and they scored 35.4%. It beat both a simple goals-per-game estimate and the league base rate.

Lineups and goalies

Lines, power-play units and starting goalies come from DailyFaceoff and refresh through the afternoon. Game-time decisions are flagged. Players with little NHL history carry a small-sample tag because their numbers lean on the positional average.

GoalIQ is a research tool, not betting advice. Probabilities are estimates and players can be scratched late. Data: NHL.com, DailyFaceoff.
